About Joyce Y.

Joyce Yin is a Data Scientist and Urban Technologist based on Hong Kong. With a bachelor's in data science and a master's in urban informatics, along with multiple research experience on urban-scal problems, She is committed to urban analytics. Her academic and professional work brought her a passion about transforming heterogeneous urban-scale data into actionable insights to tackle sustainable transportation and urban mobility issues. Advancing AI technologies in diverse experiences further motivated her to investigate how AI tools can bonus real-world problems.
